Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is CampusCash free to use?

You can create an account and use the current CampusCash student finance tools for free.

Who can see my financial records?

Your account is designed so your financial records are accessible only to you. Row Level Security policies enforce this in the database.

Does CampusCash connect to my bank or M-Pesa?

No live bank or mobile-money connection is implemented. You can manually record transactions; never share your PIN or banking credentials here.

Can I use my account on another device?

Yes. Sign in with the same account to access your saved profile and transaction history.
