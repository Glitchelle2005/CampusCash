The 50/30/20 Campus Rule

50% Survival Needs (KES 20,000)

Hostel rent, mess food, essential toiletries, medicals.

30% Academic & Study (KES 12,000)

Printing, handouts, Wi-Fi bundles, lab equipment.

20% Savings & Hustle Capital (KES 8,000)

Emergency safety cushion or invested in Money Market Funds.
